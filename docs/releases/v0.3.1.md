The first Research Bot release with the Android app.

## What's new

- **Android app.** The full app on Android 8.0 and later (built for Android 16): projects, notes with undo and redo, the four agents, ChatGPT sign-in, Crossref source search, research plans, grammar review and export. It shares its backend with the desktop app, so both behave the same way. It updates itself from GitHub Releases after checking the new APK's checksum and signing key.
- **Phone layout.** A bottom bar for Notes, Assistants, Sources, Plan and History; larger text and touch targets; sources as cards; dialogs as bottom sheets; a compact header. Everything respects the status bar, gesture bar and display cutouts.
- **Motion.** Screens, lists, dialogs and confirmations animate gently. Turned off when the system asks for reduced motion.
- **Easier sign-in.** "Sign in with ChatGPT" on the welcome screen, in the top bar and next to each agent.
- **Fixes.** Closing the account dialog cancels a pending sign-in; Android saves are batched and retried after a failed write; updates re-download if Android clears them; notifications, the drawer and the bottom bar behave correctly on phones and in split screen.

## Install

- **Android:** `research-bot-0.3.1-android.apk`. Open it on your phone and allow installs from your browser when asked. See [docs/android.md](https://github.com/Srimi1/research------bot/blob/main/docs/android.md).
- **Windows:** `research-bot-0.3.1-win-x64-setup.exe`, or let an installed copy update itself.
- **Linux:** `research-bot-0.3.1-linux-x86_64.AppImage`, or let an installed copy update itself.

Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS-android.txt`, `SHA256SUMS-linux.txt` and `SHA256SUMS-windows.txt`.
